## Active skill constraints (from the brief, with overrides)

Per the active taste-skill + minimalist-skill rules:
- **Banned fonts (default):** Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Fraunces, Instrument_Serif. **Override:** Inter IS acceptable on this project (Linear-style mission-control aesthetic explicitly approved by brief; existing Inter load in `index.html` predates this change).
- **Banned icons:** Lucide, FontAwesome, Heroicons. (No icons used in the dashboard; status pills are CSS-only.)
- **Banned shapes:** `rounded-full` for non-button containers.
- **Banned shadows:** `shadow-md` / `shadow-lg` / `shadow-xl`.
- **Banned color:** warm-cream + brass + oxblood palette, AI purple/blue glow.
- **Banned defaults:** centered hero with 6-line H1 wrap, infinite-loop micro-animations, generic 3-column Bootstrap grid.
- **No emoji** in code, markup, or visible text.
- **WCAG AA contrast** minimums.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency` fallbacks** for motion and glassmorphism.
